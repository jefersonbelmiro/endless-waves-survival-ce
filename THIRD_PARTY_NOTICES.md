## Audio

The original music and sound effects were removed from the repository for
licensing reasons. Every audio file is now a silent placeholder with the same
filename, so the game still runs and nothing breaks. If you want sound, you
need to add your own audio files.

The sources that were identified before removal are listed here so you can
re-obtain them under their own terms if you want.

### Sound effects

- Juhani Junkala, "The Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection"
  (512 sounds). Released under CC0. Safe to use and redistribute.
- "Ultimate SFX Bundle - HD Remaster". Commercial pack. Removed, do not
  redistribute.
- Other SFX had unknown origins and were removed.

### Music
